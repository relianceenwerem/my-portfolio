### Impact reporting and dashboards for nonprofits

![Reliance Enwerem](/assets/friendly-image-git.jpg){: .intro-photo}

I help nonprofits make sense of their program data and report it clearly: dashboards that staff actually open, and impact reports a funder can read in a few minutes.

My background is in strategic information, the monitoring and reporting side of program work. At ICAP at Columbia University I built visual reports in Power BI and ran the interviews and focus groups behind an HIV prevention communication strategy. Before that, at Excellence Community Education Welfare Scheme, I drafted technical reports and concept notes for USAID-funded projects. I now volunteer with the Information & Planning Analytics Unit of the American Red Cross, supporting data analysis for emergency response.

I'm also a Doctoral Fellow in Texts & Technology at the University of Central Florida. My research looks at how people make sense of risk and data, and it shapes how I present numbers to readers who don't work with them every day.

---

### What I do for nonprofits

* Review how your data is collected and reported today, and name the few fixes that would save the most staff time.
* Build a live dashboard in Power BI or Looker Studio, on free tools your organization keeps, and train your team to update it.
* Handle recurring reporting: monthly data updates, quarterly funder reports and a first draft of the annual report.
* Turn around a funder report when a deadline is close.

I work with aggregate data wherever possible, store it securely and delete client files when a contract ends.

### What I bring

A small organization would usually need two or three people to cover this work: one to set indicators and clean the data, one to build the dashboard, and one to write the report. I do all of it, and I can put the results on a simple public web page if that helps.

I'm comfortable away from the spreadsheet too. Much of my strategic information work meant sitting with program staff and participants, and I know from my own experience what it is like to arrive in a new country and rely on the services built for newcomers.

### Research

My research takes a transnational approach to risk and health communication, digital rhetoric and computational methods. I'm currently studying interface design in Nigerian-led telemedicine platforms and how it shapes the way people understand health risk. I'm always glad to hear from academic partners and public health organizations.
