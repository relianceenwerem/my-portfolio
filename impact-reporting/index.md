## Impact reporting and dashboards

Most community organizations are asked to prove their impact with data, and most don't have anyone whose job is data. Program numbers end up in several spreadsheets, each funder wants a different report, and every deadline costs program staff days of copying and checking.

I take that work off their desks. I set up the indicators, clean the data, build a dashboard the team can keep using, and write the reports.

### Who I work with

* Small community and newcomer-serving nonprofits that report to several funders and have no data or evaluation staff.
* Evaluation firms and independent consultants who need help with data cleaning, analysis or dashboards on a project.
* Foundations that want to run a data clinic or workshop for their grantees.

### How I work

We start with a short call about what you report and to whom. From there I usually suggest a health check, since it shows quickly where the time goes. Dashboards are built on free tools, so your organization owns them and isn't tied to a subscription or to me.

On data: I work with aggregate figures wherever I can, keep files in secure storage, sign a confidentiality agreement, and delete your data when the contract ends.
